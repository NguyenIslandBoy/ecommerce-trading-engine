What the blended metric says
Repeat orders as a share of all orders, by month · 0–35% scale
● Monthly repeat rate Settles at ~24% from Oct-2024 and stays there
What the cohorts say
Share of each cohort ordering again within 90 days · same 0–35% scale
● 90-day repeat rate by cohort 31.8% → 0.0%, all fully observed
Why both are correct
The denominators differ
Looks like
Repeat rate steady at 24%. Retention is fine.
Actually
That 24% is carried almost entirely by the Jul–Nov 2024 cohorts still buying. Essentially no customer acquired in 2025 returns.
Why it matters
The blended metric looks healthy until the 2024 cohorts stop buying — then revenue falls with no warning. A lagging indicator dressed as a current one.

Email engagement vs monetisation — Welcome Series
Open rate falls 22%. Conversion rate does not move.
● Open rate → 78 (−22%) ● Conversion rate → 96 (flat)
Verdict
Artifact, not lost demand
Looks like
“Engagement is collapsing across all six flows — fix the flows.”
Actually
Opens and clicks fell; orders per recipient did not. The flows monetise exactly as before — a deliverability or open-tracking change.
The rule
Engagement down + conversion flat → measurement. Engagement down + conversion down → commercial. The same test on tab 01 returns the opposite verdict for Meta.
Completeness — detected mechanically
mart_data_quality · joined by downstream metrics, not just noted
meta_ads_daily 2025-03-15
missing_day — no rows ingested
meta_ads_daily 2025-03-16
missing_day — no rows ingested
All other 1,093 source-days complete. Orders and Google Ads: zero gaps.
Why this is wired into the metric, not just reported
The difference between reporting a gap and being wrong because of one
A naive daily detector would say
“Meta spend dropped to £0 on 15–16 March — campaigns paused, investigate immediately.”
What actually happened
The rows were never ingested. Spend did not change.
The consequence if left unhandled
Blended CAC on those days computes from Google spend alone and reads £6.75 and £6.33 against a true level of ~£11.50 — a fabricated 42% improvement in acquisition cost. The metric is NULLed on incomplete days instead.
