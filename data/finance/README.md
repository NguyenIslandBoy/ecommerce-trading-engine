# Finance inputs — SYNTHETIC

**Every number in this folder is invented.** It is not company data and was never exported from a
finance system. Vendor names are generic placeholders ("Platform Meta", "Platform Google", "Agency A")
and refer to no real vendor or agency. Every row carries `is_synthetic = true`.

The files exist to give the marketing budget, pacing and reconciliation models realistic inputs to
reconcile against. They are generated by `scripts/generate_finance_inputs.py`:

- **Seed:** `20240701`. The generator is deterministic, and two runs produce byte-identical files.
- **Inputs:** `data/meta_ads_daily.csv` and `data/google_ads_daily.csv`. Budgets and platform
  invoices are sized against delivered spend so the variances look plausible.
- **Arithmetic:** money is handled in integer pence, so there is no float drift.
- **Delivered spend:** cast per row exactly as `stg_ads_daily` does.

```bash
venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/generate_finance_inputs.py
```

## Files

| File | Grain | Columns |
|---|---|---|
| `marketing_budget.csv` | month × channel × version | `budget_id, budget_month, channel, budget_version (original \| revised), approved_amount, currency, pacing_profile (linear \| front_loaded), approved_on, is_synthetic` |
| `vendor_invoice_lines.csv` | invoice line | `invoice_line_id, invoice_id, vendor, billed_channel (meta \| google \| agency), service_period_start, service_period_end, invoice_date, received_date, line_description, amount, currency, is_synthetic` |
| `invoice_line_allocations.csv` | invoice line × channel | `invoice_line_id, channel, allocation_pct, is_synthetic` |

Coverage is 2024-07 to 2025-06, matching the ad extracts. Channels are `meta`, `google` and
`tiktok`, matching `mart_daily_trading`.

### How the rows are built

- **Budgets.**
  - **Original budgets.** Every original budget is approved on the 20th of the month before.
  - **Meta and Google.** Delivered spend × a seeded factor between 0.96 and 1.10, rounded down to
    GBP 250.
  - **TikTok.** A flat GBP 1,500 a month.
- **Platform invoices.**
  - **Lines.** One line per platform per month. Each service period is one calendar month.
  - **Amount.** Delivered spend ± up to 0.35% (seeded). This stands in for the small credits and
    rounding that keep a real invoice from matching the platform UI to the penny. It sits inside
    the default 1.0% reconciliation tolerance.
  - **Allocation.** Each line has a single allocation row: 100% to its own channel.
- **Agency invoices.** A monthly retainer of GBP 2,833.33 from "Agency A", invoiced on the 1st of
  the following month and received 1 to 4 days later.

## Scenarios

The generator prints one line per scenario, with a DuckDB query that finds it.

| # | Scenario | Where | What it should produce downstream |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Forecast overspend** | Meta, 2025-05. Budget GBP 12,750. On 2025-05-15 the 7-day run rate forecasts GBP 13,539.17. Final delivered spend is GBP 13,872.57. | The pacing mart forecasts an overspend mid-month. The budget mart shows a positive variance (overspend). |
| 2 | **Delivered in M, invoiced in M+1** | Every platform month. Meta bills on the 2nd and Google on the 3rd of M+1, and the invoice is received 2 to 6 days later. Example: Meta 2025-01 is line `IL-0019`, invoiced 2025-02-02 and received 2025-02-05. | At a cursor in the first days of M+1, delivered spend is known but not yet invoiced, so an accrual appears. The recon status is `unbilled`. |
| 3 | **Agency invoice split across channels** | Every month's "Agency A" retainer line is allocated 60% to `meta` and 40% to `google`. Example: `IL-0003` (2024-07). | Allocated rows sum to the line amount to the penny. |
| 4 | **Missing cost source** | TikTok has a GBP 1,500 budget every month, but there is no TikTok cost file in the source data and there are no TikTok invoices. This is a real property of the extract, not something injected. | Delivered spend, recognised expense and variance are NULL, never 0. There is no false 100% underspend. |
| 5 | **Unallocated line** | "Agency A" 2025-02 invoice line `IL-0025`, "Creative production (one-off)", GBP 1,250.00, has **no** allocation rows. | It surfaces in an `unallocated` bucket and is never dropped. |
| + | **Recon exception (above tolerance)** | Meta 2025-03, line `IL-0026`. The platform bills GBP 11,547.01 against GBP 10,804.33 in the extract (+6.9%). | The synthetic invoice includes estimated spend for 2025-03-15 and 2025-03-16, the two days `meta_ads_daily.csv` has no rows for. The estimate is the mean of 03-14 and 03-17. The recon mart shows an `exception`, explained by the known data gap. |
| + | **Revised budget** | Google 2024-11. The original is approved 2024-10-20. A revision at ×1.15 is approved 2024-11-08. | Before 2024-11-08 only the original is visible. After it, the revised version is used. |

The reconciliation tolerance is the dbt var `recon_tolerance_pct`, default `1.0`.
